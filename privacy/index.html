Training information stays in the app

Armday works without an Armday account. Your training preferences, program, workout history, sets, weights, reps, workout dates and any body measurements you enter are stored on your device. The reviewed app does not upload that training database to Armday’s server. The app also writes a local text backup of training information and lets you export a copy through the iOS share sheet. A destination you choose when sharing, and your device-backup settings, can create additional copies outside Armday’s control.

Optional AI Reveal photos

Choosing a photo does not start an upload. Before a new Reveal, you must turn on the photo-consent control, tap Transform my arms, and then explicitly choose Allow and transform in a separate disclosure sheet; Cancel sends no new photo. The disclosure names Armday’s server and OpenAI, warns that the photo may contain sensitive information, and links to this policy. Armday creates a resized JPEG and sends it over HTTPS to its image-processing server. That server sends the photo and an Armday reference image directly to OpenAI’s API, together with an editing instruction, to generate a motivational before-and-after image. The result is not a prediction, diagnosis or guaranteed training outcome. Photos may contain faces and other personal information; the reviewed app does not create a face-identification template. This does not mean a photo is anonymous.

Where photo processing happens

The app contacts api-armday.viktigpost.se. The service is routed through Cloudflare to an operator-managed server, which sends the photo, reference image and prompt directly to OpenAI’s API. Processing is not confined to your device, Sweden or the EEA. Account-specific contractual safeguards and transfer arrangements have not been independently verified; please avoid uploading sensitive photos if this uncertainty is unacceptable.

Armday’s temporary copies

The server keeps temporary input, result and job data so a Reveal can finish and reconnect after an interruption. The app requests server-job deletion after saving its result. The reviewed server source schedules job cleanup about two hours after creation, with checks every 30 seconds while running; this is source-level behavior, not a verified production deletion deadline. Outages and cleanup failures can delay deletion. This schedule does not cover provider copies, logs or notification registrations.

OpenAI retention and training

OpenAI does not use API inputs or outputs to train its models by default; sharing for training requires an explicit opt-in. Its default abuse-monitoring logs may include customer content and are kept for up to 30 days, with longer retention possible for legal or safety reasons. Images flagged as potential child sexual abuse material may be retained for manual review. Armday has not verified special retention controls for its API account and does not promise Zero Data Retention. Deleting an Armday job does not delete OpenAI’s retained data or undo completed processing.

Photos saved on your device

The latest before-and-after pair is stored in Armday on your device until replaced, cleared with Choose another, or the app and its data are deleted. The completed Reveal folder is marked as excluded from device backup. A pending photo and job identifiers may be kept locally to resume an already-requested Reveal. If you use Save, an additional copy is added to your Photos library with permission. Clearing the Reveal inside Armday does not delete your original photo, copies saved to Photos, or copies you have shared.

Notifications and technical information

Workout reminders are scheduled locally. Remote Reveal completion messages use Apple Push Notification service. The app registers for remote notifications at launch and sends a token to Armday when iOS supplies one; registration is not limited to pressing Transform or enabling the photo-consent control. Permission to show alerts is controlled in iOS. The server keeps a separate registration list containing device tokens, push environment and update timestamps. It retains up to the latest 50 registrations, with no time-based deletion or user-facing unregister route implemented. Turning alerts off does not itself erase that list. For rate limiting, the server processes IP addresses and request times in memory. Its rolling limit looks back 24 hours, but inactive entries are not regularly purged. Server access/error logs can contain network addresses, request paths, job identifiers and diagnostic errors. A log-retention schedule has not yet been verified.

Subscriptions and purchases

Apple processes purchases. Armday uses StoreKit on your device to request products, purchase, restore purchases and read verified subscription entitlements. Armday’s reviewed backend does not receive your payment-card information or host a purchase-validation database. Apple’s handling of purchases and account information is governed by Apple’s privacy terms. Deleting app data does not cancel a subscription.

Analytics, advertising and this website

No third-party advertising, behavioral analytics or crash-reporting SDK was identified in the reviewed app source and dependencies. This does not mean no data leaves your device: image processing, notification registration, purchases and infrastructure logs are described here. This static site has no forms, client-side analytics, embedded media or third-party fonts, and its files do not set cookies. If served on GitHub Pages, GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, including for visitors who are not signed in. GitHub may process other website and service usage information under its own privacy statement. Armday does not receive those GitHub visitor logs through this static site; we have not verified GitHub’s retention period or made a claim that no other connection data is processed.

Deletion and your choices

You can decline Reveal and still use the training features. Choosing another photo resets consent for a new photo. Settings → Reset onboarding and history deletes the training database and disables scheduled workout reminders, but does not delete the separate local text backup, Reveal files, every preference, or server push registrations. Delete Armday rather than offloading it to remove its local app container; manage device backups, exports and Photos copies separately. A pending server-job deletion request is best-effort and cannot undo processing or delete data retained by OpenAI. For access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, objection or withdrawal requests, write to leo@myrenblom.se. Requests involving third-party or backup copies may have limits; we will explain those rather than promise an instant global deletion. You may complain to a supervisory authority, including Sweden’s IMY. Withdrawal does not undo processing already lawfully carried out.

When you explicitly choose an AI Reveal, the photo and associated processing are based on your consent; do not submit sensitive images unless you understand the provider and retention limits above. A photo may reveal sensitive information, and the safeguards and legal basis for such data require a specific review before inviting users to submit it.
