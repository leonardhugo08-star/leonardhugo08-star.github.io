Purchases and subscriptions

If access is missing, open the Armday paywall and tap Restore purchases using the Apple Account used for the purchase. To manage or cancel a subscription, open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Armday. Deleting Armday does not cancel a subscription. Apple handles payment and refund requests.

A Reveal is taking longer than expected

Keep your internet connection available and reopen Reveal. A pending job can reconnect without starting a new request. If the job is no longer available, select your photo and start again only if you want another generation. Do not repeatedly tap to create new generations. Completion alerts require iOS notification permission.

How to remove a Reveal

In Reveal, Choose another clears the saved before-and-after pair in Armday and resets consent for the next photo. This does not remove your original photo, copies saved in Photos or copies already processed by OpenAI. The app’s server-deletion request is separate from provider retention; see the privacy policy for the current behavior.

Exporting or resetting training history

Settings offers Export workout numbers through the iOS share sheet. Reset onboarding and history removes the training database, not all app files: the separate automatic text backup and Reveal files are not included in that reset. Delete the app rather than offloading it to remove the local app container. Keep any exported copy you need first, and manage Photos or device-backup copies separately.

Changing notifications

Use iPhone Settings → Notifications → Armday to change alert permission. Workout reminders are scheduled on-device; Reveal completion alerts use Apple’s push service. Turning notifications off does not erase an existing server registration.

Exercise safety

Stop if you experience sharp or unusual pain. Armday is not a medical diagnosis or a substitute for appropriate professional advice. AI Reveal images are motivational illustrations, not promised results.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, email leo@myrenblom.se. Include the app version, iOS version, what happened and approximately when. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or body photos in an ordinary support email.
